Method

Each period, the balance compounds at (annual return − fee) divided by the contribution frequency, then the contribution is added. Contributions rise once a year. Today's dollars divide that balance by inflation compounded from the start. Fee drag is the ending balance you would have with a zero fee, minus the balance after the fee. This is a projection, not a forecast.
