Method

P&L is the point difference times the contract multiplier times the number of contracts. Commission is $1 per contract per side, $2 round trip. Entry and exit are the lines on the chart. Prices in the list are a delayed weekday snapshot. Candles come from Yahoo and are kept for 12 hours so reloading does not ask again. This is a calculator, not a live quote or a trade signal.
